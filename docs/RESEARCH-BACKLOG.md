# Research backlog

Open extraction and verification work carried over from source v10 (2026-10-04). Items leave this list only after renewed primary-source review.

PERT needs full experimental extraction; Flow-MAE needs the complete dataset inventory and exact burst cap. Their affiliations also need stronger resolution. PTU, MM4flow and Mean MAE are now included. MT-FlowFormer appeared in 2022 and falls outside the 2023-onward additions. Bias in the Shadows is analysed from its verified January 2026 arXiv v1; its reported IWQoS proceedings record awaits accessible primary confirmation. App-Time and App-Ver need original collection documentation before separate dataset catalogue entries are added.

Further investigation is needed for temporal/cross-organisation transfer, unknown applications, calibrated confidence and false negatives, pretraining overlap and shortcut controls, minority-class robustness, and end-to-end latency/throughput/energy under load. These are evidence gaps in this corpus, not claims of field-wide absence.
